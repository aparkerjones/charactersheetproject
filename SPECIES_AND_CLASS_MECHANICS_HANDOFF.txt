PURPOSE
Continue implementing the D&D character sheet's 2014 and 2024 rules. The user wants
mechanics implemented without copying long rulebook descriptions. Apply deterministic
effects automatically; where a feature needs a die roll, ask the player to roll and
enter the result in a clear prompt. Do not claim complete coverage until every included
species/class/subclass mechanic has actually been implemented and validated.

USER SCOPE / REQUIREMENTS
- Include all playable links on the two indexes, including setting-specific and UA
  2014 entries.
- Species and mechanics data are bundled in the app for offline viewing.
- Implement mechanics, not copied prose. Keep summaries terse and paraphrased.
- Automatically calculate deterministic results. Prompt the player for required rolls.
- 2014 and 2024 are distinct rulesets; do not blend their progression or effects.
- Static JSON is fine for a name roster; TypeScript is the current home for typed
  mechanics and calculation logic. No database is currently needed.
- Species cards should all have equal height equal to the tallest card's natural
  content height, not a fixed pixel height; desktop should display up to three cards
  per row.
- Several species have subraces/lineages (notably elf, dwarf, gnome, tiefling).
  Make applicable choices explicit and persist them with the character.

Fighting Style and Expertise choices are now wired through character creation,
editing, level-up prompts, character persistence, and the class panel on the sheet.
Choice data is in optional Character.classChoices and the saved Draft.classChoices
map. Keys use `fighting-style:<classId>`, `fighting-style-cantrips:<classId>`, and
`expertise:<classId>`. Class levels enforce 2014/2024 Fighter, Paladin, Ranger,
Bard, and Rogue thresholds. 2024 Ranger Canny adds the chosen skill proficiency
and Expertise; skill bonuses use the existing doubled-proficiency calculation.
Fighting styles include the class-eligible PHB styles plus the listed supplemental

The selected cantrips are displayed, but spell casting is not yet implemented.

Fighting Style benefits are shown on the sheet but are not automatically calculated:
the app does not yet model equipment/armor or weapon attacks. Defense's armor
condition is called out rather than granting an unconditional AC bonus.
